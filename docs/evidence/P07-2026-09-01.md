## Model contract and independent reference values

P07 preserves P06's fixed 1000-hour, no-repair cooling boundary. The FMEA modes shared supply absent
(`S`), Pump A no flow (`A`), and Pump B no flow (`B`) become success blocks. P06's healthy-companion
pump effects establish that either pump is sufficient, while shared supply loss alone establishes
that every success path needs the one supply:

```text
cooling success = S works AND (A works OR B works)
```

For P06 failure inputs `q=[0.005,0.10,0.10]`, block reliabilities are
`[R_S,R_A,R_B]=[0.995,0.90,0.90]`. Under the explicit assumption that the three basic block events
are independent:

```text
R_pumps  = R_A + (1-R_A)R_B                = 0.99000
R_system = R_S R_pumps                     = 0.98505
Q_system = (1-R_S)+R_S(1-R_A)(1-R_B)       = 0.01495
```

The failure result is P05's exact top-event probability under the same boundary and assumptions.
P06 detection coverage does not enter the RBD because observation alone does not change physical
success without a modeled detection-and-recovery action.

Independent Python reference arithmetic retained in the focused tests covers:

- the five disjoint outcomes `[0.00500,0.00995,0.08955,0.08955,0.80595]`, which
  sum to one; the first two sum to `Q_system`, and the final three sum to `R_system`;
- minimal success paths `P(SA)=P(SB)=0.89550`, their actual shared intersection
  `P(SAB)=0.80595`, raw sum `1.79100`, and inclusion-exclusion result `0.98505`;
- shared-supply sweep `R_S=[0,0.50,0.90,0.995,1]`, which gives
  `R_system=[0,0.495,0.891,0.98505,0.99]` while the pump group stays `0.99`;
- Pump-A sweep `R_A=[0,0.25,0.50,0.75,1]`, which gives
  `R_system=[0.8955,0.920375,0.94525,0.970125,0.995]` while `R_S` and `R_B`
  remain fixed;
- the broken redraw at `R_S=0.80,R_A=R_B=0.90`: the correct one-supply result is `0.7920`,
  the false independent-path result is `0.9216`, the overstatement is `0.1296`, and the raw path
  sum is `1.44`;
- all eight Boolean states, block/path symmetry and limiting cases, plus directly retained tiny
  success and failure contributions.
