## Model contract and independent reference values

For marginal channel hazard `lambda >= 0` in failures/hour, dimensionless common-cause fraction
`beta` in `[0,1]`, integer channel count `n >= 1`, and mission time `t` in hours:

```text
lambda_c = beta*lambda
lambda_i = (1-beta)*lambda

R_system(t) = exp(-lambda_c*t) *
              [1-(1-exp(-lambda_i*t))^n]

Q_common(t)      = 1-exp(-lambda_c*t)
Q_independent(t) = exp(-lambda_c*t)*(1-exp(-lambda_i*t))^n
Q_system(t)      = Q_common(t)+Q_independent(t)
```

`Q_common` and `Q_independent` partition endpoint mission states into “a shared event occurred” and
“no shared event occurred, but every independent channel mode failed.” The simple all-channel
teaching model assumes identical constant hazards, one-out-of-`n` success, no repair, perfect
coverage, and one shared cause. It does not model partial-subset causes or empirical dependence.

Independent Python reference arithmetic retained by
`test_reference_values_limits_and_broken_case_are_independently_known`:

- Baseline `lambda=0.0001 /h`, `beta=0.05`, `n=2`, `T=1000 h`:
  `lambda_c=0.000005 /h`, `lambda_i=0.000095 /h`,
  `R(T)=0.9868401780159008`, `Q(T)=0.013159821984099238`,
  `Q_common(T)=0.004987520807317687`,
  `Q_independent(T)=0.008172301176781551`, and an independence-assumed
  `Q(T)=0.009055917006062713`.
- `beta=0` recovers ordinary independent redundancy. `beta=1` makes every channel count equal
  one channel, `exp(-lambda*t)`. One channel also recombines the split rates to that same marginal
  reliability for any beta.
- Zero hazard gives `R=1`, `Q=0`. At a tiny shared exposure of `2e-19`, `-expm1(-2e-19)` remains
  positive rather than being lost by direct subtraction.
- The paired log-domain calculation preserves both representable sides of the probability pair.
  At exposure `40` with 128 independent channels, `Q=0.9999999999999994` and
  `R=5.437893446773233e-16`, with `Q+R=1` in double precision. At exposure `746`, an individual
  channel's double-precision survival has underflowed to zero, while log-domain aggregation retains
  the representable 128-channel group survival `exp(log(128)-746)`.
- Broken case `lambda=0.0001 /h`, `beta=0.05`, `n=6`, `T=1000 h`: correct
  `Q(T)=0.004988072092139172`; the independence claim is `7.426724285218982e-7`, an optimism
  factor of about `6716.38`.
- For the channel-count sweep `n=[1,2,3,4,6]`, the shared-event term remains exactly
  `0.0049875208073176872` in the independent Python reference. The independent-exhaustion term
  decreases from `0.090175061156722747` at one channel to `5.5128482148475274e-7` at six
  channels, so the total decreases without attributing any channel-count benefit to the shared
  event.

These values independently check the intended equations but do not prove MATLAB numerical fidelity.
