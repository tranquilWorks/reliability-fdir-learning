## Model contract and independent reference values

For one fixed 1000-hour no-repair mission, let `S` be shared supply loss and let `A` and `B` be
pump-specific failures. `S`, `A`, and `B` are independent in this teaching calculation. Every
input is a dimensionless probability in `[0,1]` over that same mission boundary:

```text
T = S OR (A AND B)
minimal cut sets = {S}, {A,B}

qT = qS + (1-qS)*qA*qB
   = qS + qA*qB - qS*qA*qB
```

`qS` and `(1-qS)qAqB` are non-overlapping accounting contributions. The raw cut-set
probabilities `qS` and `qAqB` overlap when all three events occur and therefore are not causal
fractions that may always be summed.

Independent Python reference arithmetic retained by focused tests:

- Baseline `qS=0.005`, `qA=qB=0.10`: raw `{A,B}` probability `0.01`, shared contribution
  `0.005`, no-shared/dual-pump contribution `0.00995`, exact top-event probability `0.01495`, and
  cooling-available probability `0.98505`.
- Shared-supply sweep `qS=[0,0.005,0.02,0.05]` at fixed `qA=qB=0.10` gives
  `qT=[0.01,0.01495,0.0298,0.0595]`.
- Pump-A sweep `qA=[0,0.05,0.10,0.20,0.40]` at fixed `qS=0.005`, `qB=0.10` gives
  `qT=[0.005,0.009975,0.01495,0.0249,0.0448]`.
- Boolean states `000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111` in `SAB` order give correct top states
  `0,0,0,1,1,1,1,1`. The broken pump-OR gate falsely triggers states `001` and `010`.
- Broken baseline `S OR A OR B` gives `0.19405`, an overstatement of `0.17910` and more than ten
  times the correct probability.
- At `qS=qA=qB=0.8`, the raw cut-set sum is the impossible-as-a-probability value `1.44`, while
  exact inclusion-exclusion remains bounded at `0.928`.
- With `qS=0` and `qA=qB=1e-150`, the independently evaluated AND path remains representable as
  `1e-300`.

These values check the intended equations independently but do not prove MATLAB numerical fidelity.
